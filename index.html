Core IGCSE notes

Viruses

Not cells. They contain DNA or RNA surrounded by a protein capsid. Some have a lipid envelope.

Exam: antibiotics do not treat viral infections because viruses lack bacterial targets.

Bacteria

Single-celled prokaryotes. They contain cytoplasm, cell membrane, cell wall, ribosomes, circular DNA and sometimes plasmids or a capsule.

Reproduction: binary fission.

Prokaryote vs eukaryote

Bacteria have no nucleus or membrane-bound organelles. Animal and plant cells have nuclei and membrane-bound organelles.

Key: viruses are neither prokaryotes nor eukaryotes because they are not cellular.
