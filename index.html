📚 Learn

Viruses

  • Not made of cells.
  • Contain DNA or RNA.
  • Genetic material is enclosed by a protein capsid.
  • Some have a lipid envelope.
  • Replicate using a host cell's machinery.
Exam trap: A virus is not a prokaryote or eukaryote.

Bacteria

  • Single-celled prokaryotes.
  • No nucleus.
  • DNA is usually a circular chromosome.
  • May contain plasmids.
  • Reproduce by binary fission.
Key term: prokaryote = cell without a nucleus.

Antibiotics & resistance

Antibiotics target bacterial structures or processes. A resistant bacterium can survive treatment and reproduce. Overuse or incorrect use increases selection for resistance.

Do not write: “the bacteria become immune because they need to.” Explain survival and reproduction of resistant bacteria.
